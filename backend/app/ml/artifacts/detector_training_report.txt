KEY FINDING (worth knowing before extending this work)
  Bounding-box LOCALIZATION converges fast and is qualitatively excellent
  even at 15 epochs -- visual inspection at a low confidence threshold
  (~0.02) shows the model draws a tight box around essentially every text
  line on the label. CLASSIFICATION (which field each box belongs to) is
  the bottleneck: several classes are still being confused with "mrp" and
  raw confidence scores are low across the board (a real detection often
  scores 2-5%, not the 25%+ a production confidence threshold would use).

  This is the expected shape of the problem, not a bug: localization is a
  simpler learning task than 9-way classification, and 48 training images
  is a small dataset for a 9-class problem. It also means the path to a
  usable detector is well understood: more epochs and more (ideally real,
  not just synthetic) labeled images should specifically improve the
  classification head, since localization is already most of the way there.

RECOMMENDATION FOR PRODUCTION USE
  Do not rely on this checkpoint's class predictions yet. It's disabled by
  default in cv_service.py (see USE_TRAINED_DETECTOR). To get it
  production-ready: (1) collect 200+ real photographed packaging images,
  (2) annotate in the same YOLO format (Roboflow/CVAT/LabelImg all export
  this directly), (3) mix with or replace the synthetic set, (4) train for
  80-150 epochs, (5) re-evaluate per-class mAP before trusting any class's
  predictions in the compliance pipeline.
